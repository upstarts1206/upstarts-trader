# Lesson 28 - Position Sizing

## Objective

Calculate the correct position size based on account risk.

---

## Formula

Risk Amount = Account Balance × Risk %

Stop Distance = |Entry − Stop Loss|

Position Size = Risk Amount ÷ Stop Distance

---

## Why?

Professional traders manage risk first.

Position size is the result of the risk plan—not the starting point.
