# Lesson 44 - Trade Filtering

## Objective

Display only trade candidates that satisfy predefined criteria.

---

## Architecture

Watchlist

↓

Scanner

↓

Trade Filter

↓

Presentation

---

## Why?

Scanning and filtering are different responsibilities.

The scanner analyzes every market.

The filter decides which results are worth showing.

---

## Future Improvements

- Minimum Confidence
- Minimum Confluence
- Minimum Risk/Reward
- Session Filtering
- Strategy Filtering

---

## Key Takeaway

Always separate data collection from decision rules.
