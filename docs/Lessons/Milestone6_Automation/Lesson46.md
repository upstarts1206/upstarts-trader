# Lesson 46 - Trade Ranking

## Objective

Prioritize trade candidates by overall quality before presenting them.

---

## Architecture

Watchlist

↓

Scanner

↓

Trade Filter

↓

Trade Ranker

↓

Presentation

---

## Why?

Filtering identifies valid trades.

Ranking determines which valid trade deserves attention first.

---

## Initial Ranking Factors

- Decision Confidence
- Confluence Score
- Risk/Reward

---

## Future Improvements

- Strategy-specific ranking
- Market regime weighting
- Session weighting
- Liquidity weighting
- AI-assisted ranking

---

## Key Takeaway

Ranking is about prioritization, not just ordering.
