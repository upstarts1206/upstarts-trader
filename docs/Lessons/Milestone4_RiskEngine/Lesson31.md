# Lesson 31 - Trade Planner

## Objective

Combine all analysis into a single trade plan.

---

## Components

- Market State
- Signal
- Risk Analysis
- Entry
- Stop Loss
- Take Profit
- Position Size

---

## Why?

Instead of reading multiple outputs, traders should receive one complete trading plan.

---

## Future Improvements

- Trade Decision
- Trade Notes
- Confluence Summary
- Session Awareness
- Strategy Name
- Trade Tags

---

## Key Takeaway

A Trade Planner transforms analysis into an actionable trading plan.
