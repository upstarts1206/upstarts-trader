# Lesson 27 - Risk Engine Foundation

## Objective

Introduce the first version of the Risk Engine.

---

## Responsibilities

The Risk Engine evaluates a proposed trade.

It does not generate trading signals.

---

## Current Features

- Risk Amount
- Risk/Reward Ratio
- Trade Validation

---

## Future Features

- Position Sizing
- Dynamic Stop Loss
- Dynamic Take Profit
- ATR Stops
- Multi-Target Exits
- Trailing Stops

---

## Key Takeaway

Generating a signal and managing risk are separate responsibilities.
