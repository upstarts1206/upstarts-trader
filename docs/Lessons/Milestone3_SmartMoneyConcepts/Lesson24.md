# Lesson 24 - Fair Value Gap

## Objective

Detect basic bullish and bearish Fair Value Gaps.

---

## Bullish FVG

Low of Candle C > High of Candle A

---

## Bearish FVG

High of Candle C < Low of Candle A

---

## Current Scope

Detects simple three-candle imbalances.

Future lessons will introduce displacement filters and mitigation logic.

---

## Key Takeaway

Fair Value Gaps represent market imbalances that may later act as areas of interest.
