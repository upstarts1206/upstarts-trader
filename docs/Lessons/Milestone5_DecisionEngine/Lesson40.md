# Lesson 40 - Weighted Decision Engine

## Objective

Replace equal-weight decision rules with a weighted scoring model.

---

## Decision Weights

- Trend: 30
- Signal: 25
- Risk: 25
- Confluence: 10
- Session: 10

---

## Why?

Not every trading factor has the same importance.

A weighted model more closely reflects how experienced traders evaluate opportunities.

---

## Future Improvements

- Strategy-specific weights
- Adaptive weights
- AI-assisted weight optimization
- User-configurable weights

---

## Key Takeaway

A decision engine should prioritize the most important trading factors instead of treating every rule equally.
