# Lesson 33 - Confidence Engine

## Objective

Separate confidence scoring from trade decision logic.

---

## Current Weights

Trend : 40%

Signal : 35%

Risk : 25%

---

## Why?

Not all trading conditions contribute equally.

Weighted confidence provides more realistic decision-making than equal scoring.

---

## Future Improvements

- Strategy-specific weights
- User-defined weights
- Market session weighting
- Volatility weighting
- News weighting

---

## Key Takeaway

A confidence score should reflect the relative importance of each trading factor, not simply count how many conditions passed.
