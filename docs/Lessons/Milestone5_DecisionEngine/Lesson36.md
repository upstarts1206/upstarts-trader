# Lesson 36 - Session Engine

## Objective

Determine the current trading session.

---

## Sessions

- Asia
- London
- New York
- After Hours

---

## Why?

Different market sessions exhibit different volatility and liquidity characteristics.

Knowing the current session allows future decision rules to consider market timing.

---

## Future Improvements

- London/New York overlap
- User timezone conversion
- Session-specific strategies
- Session volatility profiles
- Weekend detection

---

## Key Takeaway

A good trading setup is not only about price—it is also about timing.
