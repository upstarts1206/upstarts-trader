# Lesson 38 - Liquidity Sweep

## Objective

Detect when price sweeps liquidity before reversing.

---

## Current Detection

- Buy-side liquidity sweep
- Sell-side liquidity sweep

---

## Why?

Institutional traders often seek liquidity before moving price in the intended direction.

Detecting sweeps helps avoid chasing breakouts and improves trade timing.

---

## Future Improvements

- Equal High / Equal Low detection
- Internal liquidity
- External liquidity
- Multi-candle sweeps
- Multi-timeframe liquidity

---

## Key Takeaway

Liquidity sweeps often precede significant market moves and provide valuable confluence for trade entries.
