# Lesson 37 - Premium & Discount

## Objective

Determine whether price is currently trading in Premium or Discount relative to the most recent market swing.

---

## Rules

Discount

Price below equilibrium.

Premium

Price above equilibrium.

Equilibrium

Exactly at the midpoint.

---

## Why?

Professional traders prefer buying at a discount and selling at a premium.

This concept helps avoid chasing price after large moves.
