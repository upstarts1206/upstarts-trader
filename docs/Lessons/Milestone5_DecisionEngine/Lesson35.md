# Lesson 35 - Confluence Engine

## Objective

Measure how many independent trading factors support the current setup.

---

## Current Confluence Factors

- Trend
- Trading Signal
- Risk Validation
- Confirmed BOS
- Bullish Fair Value Gap

---

## Why?

Professional traders rarely rely on a single signal.

A confluence score summarizes the overall quality of the trading setup by combining multiple independent factors.

---

## Future Improvements

- Liquidity Sweeps
- Order Blocks
- Premium / Discount Zones
- Session Alignment
- Multi-Timeframe Confluence
- Strategy-specific confluence

---

## Key Takeaway

A trade supported by multiple independent pieces of evidence is generally stronger than one supported by a single indicator.
