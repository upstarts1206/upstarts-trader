# Lesson 21 - Market State

## Objective

Translate technical indicators into meaningful market descriptions.

---

## Why?

Indicators are measurements.

Traders think in concepts.

Market State bridges the gap.

---

## Current States

- Trend
- Momentum
- Strength

---

## Benefits

- Easier strategy development
- Easier debugging
- Easier AI integration
- Human-readable output

---

## Key Takeaway

Applications should reason about the market instead of exposing only raw numbers.
