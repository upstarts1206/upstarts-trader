# Lesson 15 - Confirmed Break of Structure

## Objective

Support two BOS methodologies.

---

## Immediate BOS

Occurs when price closes above the latest confirmed Swing High.

---

## Confirmed BOS

Occurs after an Immediate BOS is followed by a newly confirmed Swing High.

---

## Why?

Different traders define BOS differently.

The system supports both approaches so strategies can choose which one to use.

---

## Key Takeaway

The platform should support multiple trading methodologies instead of enforcing one interpretation.
