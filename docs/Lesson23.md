# Lesson 23 - Liquidity Sweep

## Objective

Detect simple liquidity sweeps around Swing Highs and Swing Lows.

---

## Bullish Sweep

Price trades above the latest Swing High but closes back below it.

---

## Bearish Sweep

Price trades below the latest Swing Low but closes back above it.

---

## Why?

Failed breakouts often indicate liquidity collection before the real move.

---

## Current Scope

This lesson only detects Swing-based liquidity sweeps.

Future lessons will expand to session highs, equal highs/lows, and major liquidity zones.

---

## Key Takeaway

A breakout is not always a continuation. Sometimes it is simply liquidity being collected.
